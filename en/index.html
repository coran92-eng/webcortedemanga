Frequently asked questions

Where is Corte de Manga? +

Corte de Manga is at Carrer del Comte d'Urgell 108, in the Eixample neighbourhood of Barcelona. Close to Urgell metro (L1) and Hospital Clínic (L5). One of the most highly rated gastrobars in the Eixample.

What are the opening hours? +

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 08:00 to 01:00. The kitchen serves from 08:00 to 23:00: breakfast, lunch, a snack or dinner. After 23:00 we stay open for drinks and cocktails.

Can I book a table? +

Yes, we accept reservations. You can book directly from our website by clicking "Book a Table", or by calling +34 623 216 562. We especially recommend booking for weekend dinners.

What type of food do you serve? +

We are a tapas gastrobar in Barcelona specialising in Spanish and Mediterranean market cuisine. Our menu includes artisan tapas, homemade omelettes, croquettes, premium tinned fish and signature cocktails. Everything made with fresh ingredients and care.

How can I contact Corte de Manga? +

You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp at +34 623 216 562, or by email at info@cortedemanga.com. You can also message us on WhatsApp using the floating button on the website.

How to get here

We are in the heart of the Eixample neighbourhood of Barcelona, in the Esquerra de l'Eixample area. If you are looking for a tapas bar in Barcelona with market cuisine and a gastrobar atmosphere, we are just a few minutes' walk from several central points.
