Early breakfast in Barcelona is harder than it looks

If you work near Hospital Clínic, start at 08:00, or have a train to catch, you already know: there are far fewer options first thing than you'd expect. A lot of Eixample bars open at 09:00 or 09:30, and at weekends plenty don't open until mid-morning.

The usual result is that you end up in a chain, with mediocre coffee, because it's the only thing open.

We open at 08:00, seven days a week, and the kitchen runs from that same hour. It's not a promotion or a seasonal thing — it's the year-round schedule.

Weekday breakfast or Sunday brunch

They're two different things, and both work here.

During the week, most people who come in early want something quick: coffee, something to eat, twenty minutes sitting down, then on with the day. The bar is there for exactly that.

At the weekend the rhythm changes. People come down later, stay longer and eat more. Because the kitchen is open from 08:00 and doesn't stop until 23:00, there's no cut-off point where breakfast "ends" and you get moved on — you can let it drift into vermouth hour without changing tables.

From the menu, what works best mid-morning is the coca bread with tomato and olive oil (€4.50) and our omelettes, made to order, from €6.90 for the classic to €8.90 for Iberian ham and black truffle. And if it's a slow Sunday, there's mimosa at €4.

You can see the full menu with prices before you come.

Terrace, laptops and dogs

Three things we get asked a lot at this hour:

  • Terrace: yes. Outside tables are first come, first served — they can't be booked.
  • Wifi: yes, free. Early on there's usually plenty of room to work for a while.
  • Dogs: welcome.

The venue is also wheelchair accessible. That and a few more things are in the FAQ.

Getting here

We're at Carrer del Comte d'Urgell 108, in the Esquerra de l'Eixample. The handiest stop is Urgell (L1); Hospital Clínic (L5) is a couple of minutes' walk. Buses 9, 27, 54 and 56.
