Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum group size? +

From 4 people. The Taula Plena menu requires a minimum of 8.

Does everyone have to choose the same menu? +

Yes, the whole group shares the same menu level. Then each person chooses their own main and dessert.

Do you have vegetarian options? +

Yes. The mushroom cannelloni and several starters are vegetarian. Let us know and we'll adapt.

Are drinks included? +

No, drinks are ordered separately.

Can we mention allergies or intolerances? +

Of course. Let us know when booking and we'll take it into account.
