Hours and bookings

How late does the kitchen serve food?

The kitchen is open from 08:00 to 23:00 every day. The venue stays open until 01:00, but after 23:00 we only serve drinks and cocktails.

So: if you're coming for dinner, be seated before 23:00. If you're coming for a drink after eating somewhere else, you have until 01:00.

What are your opening hours?

We're open every single day, Monday to Sunday, 08:00 to 01:00. We don't close on any weekday.

Do I need to book?

Not compulsory — we always keep space for walk-ins. But Thursday to Saturday evenings fill up, so if you want a specific time or you're more than two people, book ahead.

Book on the website, on WhatsApp, or call +34 623 216 562.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at +34 623 216 562 with the name and time of the booking. Please tell us as early as you can if you can't make it, so we can give the table to someone else.

Do you serve breakfast? Also at weekends?

Yes. We open at 08:00 every day, Saturdays and Sundays included. It's one of the things that sets us apart in this part of town.

The venue

Do you have a terrace?

Yes. We can't guarantee it when you book because it depends on the weather and how busy we are: terrace tables are first come, first served. Tell us when you arrive and we'll do what we can.

Is it wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the venue is wheelchair accessible.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are welcome.

Is there wifi?

Yes, free wifi for customers. Ask at the bar for the password.

Can I smoke?

Not indoors, as required by Spanish law. On the terrace, yes.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We serve in Spanish, Catalan and English, and the menu is available in all three.

Food, menu and prices

How much does it cost to eat here?

Tapas run from €3 (the gildas) to €25 (hand-carved Iberian ham), with most dishes between €8 and €15.

As a rule of thumb: four or five dishes shared between two people is plenty. With drinks, expect €25–40 per person depending on what you order.

Do you have a set lunch menu?

No menú del día. We serve à la carte. For groups of 4 or more we do have set menus from €32.

What should I order first time?

The three that leave the kitchen most:

  • Confit Tudela artichokes with Iberian ham and egg yolk (€19)
  • Pork cheek braised in red wine with creamy mash (€17.90)
  • Burrata Secchiello

For something quicker: the handmade croquettes (Iberian ham, cod, oxtail) and the Iberian ham and black truffle omelette.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. Tell us when you book or when you arrive and we'll walk you through what's available that day — it depends on the market.

Do you have gluten-free options? I'm coeliac.

No. We'd rather be straight about it: we can't currently guarantee gluten-free dishes or prevent cross-contamination in the kitchen, so we don't recommend our restaurant if you have coeliac disease.

If you have a mild intolerance, message us beforehand and we'll go through the menu with you in detail.

Do you serve cocktails?

Yes, signature cocktails: Melon Breeze (€11), Red Sky (€12) and Peach Breeze (€12), plus classics like mojito, espresso martini and paloma. Served until 01:00.

Groups and celebrations

Do you take groups? How many people?

Yes. We have set menus for groups of 4 to 15 people from €32 per person, with starters to share, a main course of your choice and dessert. For larger groups, message us and we'll see what we can do.

Can I celebrate a birthday here?

Of course. Tell us when you book so we can set the table up properly. If you're bringing a cake, let us know in advance.
